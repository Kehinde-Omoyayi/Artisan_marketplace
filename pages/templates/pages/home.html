Trust & safety

Staged verification

Every artisan climbs a five-level ladder — phone, profile, government ID, address, skill reference — before taking on higher-risk jobs like electrical or plumbing work.

Payments held, not handed over

Funds are only released once you confirm the job is done. Large payouts get a human check before they move.

A person reviews every dispute

Opening a dispute immediately pauses any pending payout and puts a real support case in front of staff — not a queue that resolves itself.
